About SAIYAN

A network built to move ideas into action.

SAIYAN is the Singapore–Sarawak AI Youth Ambassador Network. We connect young people, organisations, institutions, mentors and opportunities across borders to turn AI learning into practical action.

Learn AI. Build something real. Transform across borders.

Not just another AI programme.

SAIYAN is a cross-border youth and digital transformation network connecting Singapore and Sarawak.

Through practical learning, real challenges, mentorship and collaboration, participants develop the confidence to use AI in meaningful situations. For organisations, SAIYAN creates a pathway to engage emerging builders, explore practical innovation and support measurable digital outcomes.

SAIYAN is designed as a two-way relationship. It is not a one-way pipeline from one place to another. Both sides contribute, learn, build and grow together.

An open ecosystem.

SAIYAN may collaborate with additional universities, companies, trainers, technology partners, builders, specialists and institutions according to programme needs.

Each organisation retains its independently owned or developed expertise, methodology and intellectual property. Collaborations may evolve as the network grows.

Creating a network that can grow with its builders.

Our long-term direction is to create more ways for young people to learn, build, collaborate and access opportunity across Singapore and Sarawak.

We want SAIYAN to support practical capability development, responsible use of AI, cross-border relationships and projects that create value for real people and organisations.
