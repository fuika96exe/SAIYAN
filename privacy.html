1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Singapore-Sarawak AI Youth Ambassador Network (SAIYAN) may collect, use, store and protect personal information when people visit this website, submit a youth application, send an organisation enquiry or interact with related community services.

We are committed to handling personal data responsibly and with care, respecting the trust of our participants, mentors, partners and visitors across Singapore and Sarawak.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and programmes, information collected may include:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Location: Current location or region (e.g. Singapore, Sarawak, or other).
  • Application & Brief Details: Educational background, motivations, project interests, organisation details, or challenge statements submitted through our forms.
  • Preferences: Communication preferences and consent confirmations.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser, device type, and general website interaction logs.

We do not collect sensitive categories of personal data that are not relevant to evaluating participation or responding to enquiries.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided to SAIYAN is used solely for legitimate operational and community development purposes, including to:

  • Review and process youth cohort applications;
  • Respond to inquiries from businesses, government bodies, universities, sponsors and mentors;
  • Communicate updates about relevant workshops, hackathons, exchanges, or opportunities;
  • Improve website navigation, accessibility and user experience;
  • Maintain the security, integrity and uptime of our digital services.

We do not sell, rent or monetize your personal information to any third parties.

4. Youth Applications

When a young builder submits a form through our Youth Application portal, the information provided is reviewed by our facilitators to understand the applicant’s interests, background and eligibility for available tracks (such as SAIYAN Academy, Missions, Build, or Sailor SAIYAN).

Submitting an application expresses interest and does not guarantee automatic acceptance or cohort placement. Additional verification may be requested prior to project onboarding.

5. Organisation and Partner Enquiries

Information submitted by enterprises, SMEs, public sector institutions, educational bodies or mentors is used to understand the scope of the proposed challenge or partnership brief and determine a suitable engagement model.

Initial enquiry submission initiates exploratory discussions and does not constitute a binding commercial agreement or guaranteed project execution.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may utilize essential cookies strictly necessary for fundamental site navigation and form preservation.

The website may implement privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics tools in the future to understand general usage trends and improve performance. Any non-essential tracking or analytics will be deployed in compliance with applicable consent guidelines.

7. How We Share Information

We do not share your personal data with outside parties, except when strictly necessary to operate our platform, host secure infrastructure, or facilitate verified programme cohorts with designated mentors and organisers under strict confidentiality expectations.

Data may also be disclosed if required by applicable legal obligations, governmental requests, or to protect the safety and rights of participants.

8. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, support ongoing programme participation, resolve disputes, and maintain operational compliance. When information is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Data Security

SAIYAN applies reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

While we strive to implement robust security standards, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or request the deletion or withdrawal of consent for specific processing activities.

To make a request regarding your personal data, please contact us using the details provided below.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, community platforms, or resources operated by third parties. SAIYAN is not responsible for the privacy practices, terms, or content of external third-party websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy notices of any external site they visit.

12. Children and Young Persons

SAIYAN engages youth builders and students. For participants who are minors under applicable regional laws, additional parental or guardian consent, verification, or institutional coordination may be required depending on specific programme activities and travel requirements.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may review and update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect evolving programme practices, community requirements, or legal standards. The latest active draft will always be posted directly on this page.
